# Cinematic Quality QA — 6.5

Separate from correctness QA. Evaluate PASS / WARN / REPAIR for:
- action readability
- character distinction
- tactical clarity
- rhythm progression
- impact escalation
- camera variety without randomness
- shot readability
- environmental interaction
- emotional progression
- ending clarity
- prompt overload

No numeric or winner ranking is required. Quality QA may request repair but cannot override a correctness gate.


## Combat-First Quality Gate
When combat-first pacing is enabled, also evaluate:
- first meaningful combat event timing
- intro budget compliance
- static character presentation budget
- combat-time ratio
- action-first character introduction
- opening template suitability
- absence of redundant establishing/presentation shots

A WARN/REPAIR may compress presentation, but may not fabricate an attack or bypass physics/tactical validation.
