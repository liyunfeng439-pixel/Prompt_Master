# Damage & Environment Evolution

Track damage as persistent state rather than decorative VFX.

## Default progression
INTACT → LIGHT DAMAGE → STRUCTURAL DAMAGE → SEVERE DAMAGE → COLLAPSE/DESTRUCTION

## Track
- character: costume tears, dirt, blood/damage indicators only when explicitly allowed, posture/fatigue
- weapon: intact, chipped, damaged, broken, disarmed
- environment: cracks, fragments, displaced objects, broken surfaces, collapsed structures

## Rules
1. Damage must have a cause and timestamp/beat.
2. Damage persists until repaired by an explicit event.
3. High-energy contact with fragile or structurally compromised objects should produce immediate visible reaction; avoid artificial pause before breakage.
4. Do not escalate every impact to destruction; scale outcome to material, contact area, direction, energy and prior damage.
