# Character / Weapon / World Lock — 6.4

Lock the minimum stable facts needed to execute combat.

## Character lock
identity, silhouette, body proportions, equipment, known abilities, known limitations.

## Weapon lock
weapon identity, category, observable geometry, grip mode, reach when known, edge/blunt/energy behavior when supported.

## World lock
ground/support surfaces, major obstacles, destructible structures, height layers, traversal boundaries and camera-relevant landmarks.

## Lock policy
A field may be locked only from explicit user constraint or sufficiently supported asset evidence. Unknown fields remain UNKNOWN. Conflict blocks dependent hard claims until resolved or explicitly accepted as an uncertainty.
