# Reference Motion Extraction Runtime — 6.4

Convert a reference video into motion-neutral observations.

## Extraction
segment into preparation, acceleration, commitment, contact, response, recovery; capture trajectory, timing, weight transfer, contact timing, target relation and camera intent separately.

## Transfer boundary
Preserve motion intent, timing relationships, force-transfer logic and tactical purpose. Adapt amplitude, posture, weapon interpretation, scale, camera and VFX to target assets.

## Never transfer as FACT
Unseen anatomy, exact weapon mass, hidden collisions, or fictional powers are not extracted as facts.
