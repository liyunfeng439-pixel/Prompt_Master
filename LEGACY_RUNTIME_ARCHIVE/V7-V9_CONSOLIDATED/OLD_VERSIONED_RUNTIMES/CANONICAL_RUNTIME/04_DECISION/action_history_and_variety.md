# Action History & Variety Runtime — 6.4

Maintain recent action family, direction, defense response, movement pattern, impact pattern and camera pattern.

Apply repetition penalties to identical or near-identical patterns unless repetition is tactically intentional, stylistically locked, or part of a named combo.

Variety must not override tactical necessity or character style. Escalation should change decision pressure, consequence, speed, range, power or environmental interaction—not merely add adjectives.
