# Impact Director

Convert the requested "爽感/冲击感" into controllable production parameters instead of vague adjectives.

Parameters: IMPACT, SPEED, POWER, WEIGHT, DESTRUCTION, CAMERA_VIOLENCE, RHYTHM, RECOVERY, ESCALATION.

Each beat may set relative levels and a purpose. The director must preserve action readability: impact emphasis never overrides character lock, spatial continuity or causal physics.

For heavy hits: readable anticipation → committed trajectory → contact → immediate physical consequence → recovery/aftershock.
For rapid exchanges: shorter anticipation and recovery, but contact direction and state transitions remain explicit.
Escalation should change at least one of speed, scale, displacement, consequence, tactical risk or camera intensity rather than merely adding more VFX.


## V6.8 High-Impact / Stylish Action Priority
For combat-led videos, `IMPACT`, `SPEED`, `POWER`, `WEIGHT`, `DESTRUCTION`, `CAMERA_VIOLENCE`, `RHYTHM`, `RECOVERY`, `ESCALATION` are supplemented by action readability controls: `MOTION_AMPLITUDE`, `ACCELERATION_CHANGE`, `CONTACT_PAYOFF`, `DISPLACEMENT_PAYOFF`, `WEAPON_IDENTITY`, `COMBO_LINK`, `SILHOUETTE_READABILITY`.

High-impact preference is applied only after feasibility, continuity, tactical and asset constraints pass. Do not manufacture spectacle through VFX alone. A visually strong beat should be strong because the body/weapon interaction produces a readable result.
